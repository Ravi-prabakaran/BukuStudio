# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Hardcover covers (case laminate wrap, hinge and safe areas) and KDP's hardcover rules: 75 to 550 pages, five sizes, black and white or premium color. The wizard offers hardcover only for longer books.
- Kindle eBook as EPUB: fixed layout for picture books (pages as printed, words hidden underneath for search), reflowable for chapter books and novels.
- Covers always print in color.
- Packaging: install guide, privacy note, plugin and marketplace details.
